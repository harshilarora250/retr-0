# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Retr-0 a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, communicate constructively, assume good intent, and focus feedback on the work. Welcome different perspectives and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and disruptive or abusive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact method listed in the repository. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially where possible.
