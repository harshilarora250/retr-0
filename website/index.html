The translation layer

Retro by intent, not by filter.

Retr-0 reads the page's semantics and rebuilds its visual language: controls become controls, cards become panels, dialogs become windows, and dark interfaces find their way back to gray.

</>
Intelligent transformation

Semantic HTML, ARIA roles, class signals, and visual cues guide each treatment.

▣
Authentic controls

Bevels, focus outlines, menus, tabs, inputs, cursors, and title bars share one system.

↻
